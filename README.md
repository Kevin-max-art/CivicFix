# 🚦 CivicFix

## Community Civic Early-Warning & Prevention Platform

> **See it. Report it. Understand it. Prevent it.**

CivicFix is a student-developed prototype designed to help communities
identify recurring and emerging civic problems before they become larger
issues.

The project is designed for Tamil Nadu, with an initial focus on Chennai.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Citizens frequently notice civic problems such as:

- 🛣️ Potholes and damaged roads
- 🌧️ Waterlogging
- 🗑️ Garbage accumulation
- 🚰 Drainage problems
- 💡 Broken streetlights
- 🏗️ Damaged public infrastructure

Individual reports may show an isolated problem, but repeated reports
can reveal a larger pattern.

CivicFix focuses on identifying these patterns at the community level.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CivicFix acts as a **community-level civic intelligence and
early-warning layer**.

The system collects community signals and analyzes:

- 📍 Location
- 🏷️ Problem category
- 🔁 Recurrence
- 📊 Frequency
- 📈 Trend
- ⚠️ Severity

The prototype then highlights areas where civic problems appear to be
increasing or recurring.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Community Problem Reporting
Citizens can report a civic problem using:

- Photo
- Location
- Simple category selection
- Text
- Voice-based input

### 2. Accessible Interface

Designed for both literate and low-literacy users through:

- Large visual icons
- Simple navigation
- Tamil + English interface
- Voice interaction
- Minimal text requirement

### 3. Recurring Issue Detection

The system can identify multiple reports relating to:

- Similar locations
- Similar categories
- Repeated problems

### 4. Civic Risk Analysis

The prototype analyzes:

**Frequency + Recurrence + Trend + Severity**

to generate a civic risk level.

### 5. Civic Risk Map

The system can visualize:

🟢 Normal  
🟡 Emerging  
🟠 High Risk  
🔴 Critical

### 6. Citizen Resolution Verification

A future workflow can allow citizens to confirm whether a reported
problem has actually been resolved.

### 7. Analytics Dashboard

The dashboard can display:

- Total community signals
- Active problems
- Recurring problems
- High-risk locations
- Problem categories
- Trends

### 8. Government Handoff

CivicFix is designed to complement existing government grievance
systems rather than replace them.

---

# 🏛️ Existing Government Systems

During our research, we identified existing grievance systems such as:

- Greater Chennai Corporation grievance system
- 1913
- Namma Chennai
- Government of India's CPGRAMS
- CPGRAMS Intelligent Grievance Management System (IGMS)

These systems already provide important grievance-management,
tracking, analytics and AI/ML capabilities.

Therefore, CivicFix does **not** claim to replace these systems.

Our proposed focus is a **community-level early-warning and
prevention layer** for recurring physical civic problems.

---

# 🔍 Our Innovation

Instead of focusing only on:

> **Problem → Complaint → Resolution**

CivicFix explores:

> **Community Signals → Pattern Detection → Risk Identification
> → Preventive Recommendation → Government Handoff**

The objective is to identify emerging civic problems earlier and make
the information easier for communities to understand.

# ⚠️ Prototype Status

CivicFix is currently an **academic proof-of-concept prototype**.

The predictive and risk-analysis features demonstrated in this
project are experimental.

Real-world deployment would require:

- Historical civic datasets
- Model validation
- Security testing
- Government cooperation
- Official API/integration permissions
- Privacy and accessibility review
